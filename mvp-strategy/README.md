# MVP Fan Platform — Strategy Pack

Three deliverables for Ryan Rechten / MVP Partnerships, built around the MVP fan engagement platform and Dropt analytics dashboard.

| File | Audience | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `01-sponsor-sales-sheet.md` | External sponsors | One-pager Ryan can send to brands to sell platform-native sponsorship packages |
| `02-cmo-deck.md` | Internal MVP leadership | 6-slide deck framing the platform as a CMO operating model |
| `03-launch-checklist.md` | Internal ops team | 14-day pilot plan with owners, dates, success criteria, risk register |

**Headline thesis:** MVP currently rents fan attention from third-party platforms. The fan engagement platform + Dropt converts that attention into a first-party, opted-in, retargetable audience that compounds fight after fight — turning every event into year-round revenue and unlocking a new tier of measurable sponsorship inventory.
