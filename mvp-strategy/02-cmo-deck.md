## SLIDE 1 — The Setup

# Every fight is a 90-day attention spike.

Today MVP **rents** that attention from Meta, YouTube, and ESPN.

Tomorrow MVP **owns** it.

> *"This is how we turn one fight into a year of revenue, and one sponsor into ten."*

**Speaker note:** Open with the gap. Right now MVP generates the heat but doesn't keep the data. The platform is the asset that closes that gap.

## SLIDE 2 — The Problem (3 gaps we live with today)

| Gap | What it costs us |
|---|---|
| **Anonymous audience** | Can't email PPV buyers, can't retarget viewers, no CRM to grow |
| **Unmeasurable sponsorships** | Selling logos for $X when we could sell audiences for $5X |
| **Audience goes dark between fights** | Pay full CAC every cycle; no compounding asset |

**The big idea:** *If we can't name the fan, we can't monetize the fan twice.*

## SLIDE 3 — The Platform, in plain English

**Fan Engagement Platform** = fun fight-week interactions that capture data
- Pick the Winner / Round / Method
- Live round-by-round scorecards
- Walkout song & outfit votes
- Branded trivia + loyalty drops

**Dropt Analytics** = the dashboard that turns those interactions into decisions
- Where fans came from
- What they did
- What it was worth

**One sentence:** *Engagement on top, intelligence underneath, revenue out the side.*

## SLIDE 4 — Three Revenue Engines

### 💰 Engine 1 — Sponsorship reinvented
Sell **moments + audiences**, not logos.
"Pick the Round, Presented by Celsius" → audience file delivered to Celsius.
**Lift:** 5–10x CPM vs. broadcast.

### 🎟️ Engine 2 — Direct fan revenue
Use the captured list to drive **PPV, tickets, merch** at a fraction of cold CAC.
**Lift:** 3–5x conversion on retargeted vs. cold traffic.

### 📅 Engine 3 — Year-round monetization
Email/SMS list = launch channel for the **next** fight at near-zero CAC.
**Worth:** A clean 250K-fan list = **$2–4M/year** in addressable media + sponsor lift.

## SLIDE 5 — The CMO Operating Model

**One ratio that runs the whole company:**

> **Revenue per captured fan ÷ Cost per captured fan**

If > 1, scale spend. If < 1, fix the funnel. That's it.

**Daily 3-number Slack digest:**
1. Fans captured (24h)
2. $ cost per captured fan
3. $ revenue per captured fan

**Weekly review (in Dropt):**
- Acquisition: where the cheap fans come from → reallocate spend
- Engagement: who the whales are → tag for premium sponsor inventory
- Conversion: which screen leaks money → ship a fix

**Ask:** Greenlight a 14-day pilot on the next fight.

**Pilot scope:**
- 1 activation live (Pick the Round)

**Cost to test:** ~$0 incremental — repurposed marketing hours.
**Upside if it works:** A new revenue line we own forever.

> *"We don't need a bigger fight. We need to keep the fans we already have."*

### Appendix B — KPI definitions
- **Captured fan:** opt-in email or phone, attached to ≥1 platform action
- **Engaged action:** any logged interaction (poll, prediction, vote, score)
- **Superfan:** top decile by action count over trailing 90 days
- **Revenue per fan:** trailing 90-day attributable revenue ÷ captured fans
