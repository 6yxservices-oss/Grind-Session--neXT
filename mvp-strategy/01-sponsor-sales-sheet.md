## Why this works for boxing

- A fight is a **shared appointment** — fans show up at the same time, ready to interact
- Predictions and scorecards have **built-in reasons to come back** (round-by-round)
- Walkout votes and method polls are **share-native** — they spread organically
- The audience skews **18–34 male, high-discretionary-income** — premium for sponsors
- Repeat events compound the list — **next fight starts at 1.0x cost, not zero**
