04How it makes money BOTTOM · MONETIZE

Three revenue lines, all unlocked the moment the funnel starts running. Sponsorship leads — sweepstakes is the easiest inventory in the deck. Direct fan revenue and year-round monetization compound on top.

Sponsorship — two ways to print money on day one

Prong 1 · New inventory

Sell sweepstakes sponsorship

Sweepstakes are the easiest piece of inventory in the deck. Brands already have a sweepstakes line in their marketing budget. They know how the format works. They've bought it from broadcasters, sportsbooks, and creators for years. Now they buy it from MVP — with the audience file attached.

Why this clears procurement fast: sweepstakes sponsorship maps to an existing approval template at every CPG, sportsbook, and beverage. No new vendor category. No new legal review. Just a "Presented by" placement and a dataset at the end.
WHAT WE SELL:
  • Title sweepstakes — "Win VIP fight night, presented by [Brand]"
  • Bonus-entry mechanics — "Watch [Brand]'s 30-sec clip for +5 entries"
  • Branded prize tiers — sponsor-supplied prizes (year of Celsius, $5K travel)
  • Audience hand-off — opt-in entrants delivered to sponsor CRM post-fight
PRICING TIERS
$150K–$250K presenting · $500K–$750K fight-week · $1.5M–$3M season exclusive
Prong 2 · Extract current value

Upgrade existing sponsor deals

Most current MVP sponsors are paying for logo exposure — corner posts, ring mats, broadcast bugs. Same deal, three years running, same flat fee. Club MVP turns that flat fee into a measured asset — and a justification for renewal at 2–3x.

The renewal pitch: "Last year you paid $X for impressions. This year, same dollar buys impressions plus a sweepstakes activation, an audience file in your demo, and a measurable dwell-time report. Or for 2x, you own the activation outright."
HOW WE EXTRACT MORE:
  • Audit current deals — tag what each sponsor gets vs. could get
  • Activation upsell — first right of refusal on Club MVP activations
  • Retroactive ROI proof — retro-fit Dropt reporting on existing deals
  • Tier ladder — Logo → Logo + activation → Category exclusive
EXPECTED LIFT ON RENEWAL
+30–100% on existing sponsor revenue with zero new logos to chase

Combined: 1–2 new sweepstakes sponsors per fight ($300K–$1M) + uplift on 4–6 existing renewals (+$500K–$2M/year). Day-one impact, no platform-side risk.

Plus — direct fan revenue (this is what fills seats)

Drives this fight

  • Ticket sales — geo-segment captured fans in host city → SMS at T-72h
  • PPV bundle offers — fans who picked a winner get one-tap PPV upsell
  • Merch conversion — fan who picks Fighter A sees Fighter A's shirt at checkout
  • Lookalike audiences — pixel feeds Meta/TikTok for paid scaling at 3–5x cold conversion

Drives the next fight

  • Free re-launch channel — email/SMS the captured list, ~$0 CAC
  • Pre-sale priority — superfans get first crack at tickets (loyalty hook)
  • Off-season retention — content drops, training Q&As, sponsor newsletter inventory
  • Year-round monetization — VIP/loyalty memberships, premium sweepstakes, affiliate ticketing

Year-one upside on a 250K-fan list: $2–4M in addressable media + sponsor lift — on top of the per-fight sponsorship revenue above.

06Why this is simple

MVP doesn't build, integrate, or operate anything. The product runs on top of the website MVP already owns. The only thing MVP brings is what MVP already does best.

MVP provides (no new lift)

  • Prizes / VIP access / experiences (already in inventory)
  • Fight-week promotion across owned channels (already running)
  • Sponsor relationships (already exist)
  • Fighter participation — 15-sec selfies (already organic)
  • Live-event amplification (already happens)

Club MVP handles (everything else)

  • "Join Club MVP" button + member accounts on existing site
  • Sweepstakes logic, legal compliance, prize fulfillment
  • Sponsor integrations + branded UX
  • Data capture, CRM, lifecycle email/SMS
  • Dropt analytics, dashboards, sponsor reporting
